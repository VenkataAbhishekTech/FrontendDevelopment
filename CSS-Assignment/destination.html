About Paris

Paris, the capital city of France, is known for its art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture. The city's 19th-century cityscape is crisscrossed by wide boulevards and the River Seine.

Top Attractions

From the iconic Eiffel Tower to the historic Notre-Dame Cathedral and the world-famous Louvre Museum, Paris offers endless experiences for travelers.

French Culture

Paris is a global center for art, literature, and philosophy. The cafés, art galleries, and theaters reflect a culture deeply rooted in creativity and sophistication.

Plan Your Visit

Visit Paris in the spring or fall to enjoy pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Don't forget to try local delicacies like croissants, macarons, and fine French wine!
